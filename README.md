# 🚀 JobCopilot

**Your AI Assistant for Job Applications**

[JobCopilot](https://jobcopilot.com) is an AI-powered platform that helps job seekers land more interviews — faster. We automate your job search by applying to up to **50 relevant job listings daily** from a pool of over **300,000 companies worldwide**.

### ✨ Platform Highlights
  
✅ Automatically finds and applies to relevant jobs tailored to your profile  
✅ Users can train their Copilot to respond like them — it gets smarter over time  
✅ Built-in tracking to manage applications in one simple dashboard  
✅ Secure, hands-free, and scalable for job seekers across industries

Whether you're a fresh graduate, career switcher, or experienced professional, JobCopilot ensures your applications are delivered faster than the average job seeker — giving you the edge in a competitive market.
